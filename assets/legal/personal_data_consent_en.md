## 2. Personal Data Covered by this Consent

This Consent applies to the following information:

* the User’s name displayed in the profile and during interactions on the Platform;
* the unique internal identifier of the User’s account;
* mobile telephone number;
* the address where specialized equipment is located and where work is performed;
* approximate location, including an area selected on the interactive map;
* precise location, including the coordinates of a point selected by the User on the map;
* text messages exchanged through the Application’s built-in chats;
* photographs, including profile photographs, equipment images, and uploaded documents;
* other content created by the User, including listings, equipment specifications, comments, ratings, and reviews;
* information about the User’s actions within the Application, including creating and modifying orders, changing statuses, search history, and favorites;
* device identifiers and an FCM token used for push notifications;
* Individual Identification Number — IIN;
* technical passport or registration document for specialized equipment;
* documents confirming ownership of or the right to operate or dispose of the equipment;
* driver’s license;
* state registration number of the specialized equipment.

The IIN, equipment documents, driver’s license, and state registration number of specialized equipment are provided upon request for the verification of Equipment Owners.

This Consent does not apply to the collection of:

* biometric data, including selfies and data used for liveness verification;
* Users’ bank card details.

The Operator does not collect such information.

## 4. Term of Consent, Withdrawal Procedure, and Data Retention

### 4.1

This Consent remains valid throughout the entire period during which the User uses the PROKAT mobile application and until the User’s account is deleted.

### 4.2

The User may withdraw this Consent at any time.

Consent may be withdrawn in either of the following ways:

* by deleting the account within the Application;
* by submitting a request through the following webpage:

[auyltech.kz/prokat/account-deletion](https://auyltech.kz/prokat/account-deletion)

### 4.3

The User’s primary data will be completely deleted within 30 calendar days.

The User acknowledges and agrees that withdrawing this Consent makes further use of the Application technically impossible.

### 4.4

Following account deletion, the Operator applies the following maximum data retention periods:

* primary account data is deleted within 30 calendar days;
* standard technical security logs are retained for no longer than 6 months;
* materials relating to a specific confirmed case of fraud, a violation of the Platform rules, or a dispute may be retained until the relevant matter has been resolved, but for no longer than 12 months, unless a longer period is expressly required by the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

### 4.5

The existence of an active request does not prevent the User’s account from being deleted.

Where there is a specific unresolved dispute, security incident, or legal requirement, the Operator may temporarily retain only the minimum amount of data necessary to consider the relevant matter or comply with applicable legal obligations.
